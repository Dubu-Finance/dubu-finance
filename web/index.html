What this does and does not say.
  1. The comparison column is a Uniswap V3 pool that is not ours — 0x98e5…acfc, real WBTC/USDC, 0.30% fee. We did not seed it, do not maintain it and cannot move it. Its quotes include however far its own price has drifted from the market since it was last traded; that drift is separated out below each table for exactly that reason. An earlier version of this page compared against a UniswapV2 pair we had deployed and abandoned, and reported its 53% staleness as a cost of the constant-product curve. That was not a baseline, it was our own neglect, and it has been removed.
  2. At small size most of the honest gap is fees, not curve shape: the V3 pool charges a flat 30 bp where this pool quotes a 5 bp half-spread. The curve only starts to matter at size.
  3. The V3 figures at size are in-range figures. Between two initialized ticks a V3 pool is a constant product on virtual reserves and the closed form used here is exact. Across a tick boundary its liquidity changes and the formula stops being true, so a size that would cross is printed as out of range rather than filled in with a number this page cannot stand behind.
  4. Past its capacity epoch this pool refuses — it quotes nothing and the swap reverts — where the V3 pool keeps filling at any size, tick after tick. Declining is not strictly better than filling badly. It is a different trade-off, and it is what bounds how much a stale ladder can be picked off for.
  5. Adverse selection is not modelled. The reference is right by assumption here. These are execution-quality numbers for an honest taker, not a claim about the maker's P&L.
Both tokens on our side are mocks, and the two venues are not routable against each other: the V3 pool holds real WBTC and USDC where ours hold mocks, because GIWA has no canonical stablecoin, no Circle CCTP domain, and the third-party tokens expose no mint or faucet — that inventory would have to be bought. So nothing routes between the two sets. This is a price comparison, not an executable route. For ETH there is no comparison at all. Neither third-party V3 factory on this chain has a WETH pool (they hold WBTC/USDC, WBTC/USDT ×2 and USDC/USDT), and the only third-party WETH-against-a-stablecoin V2 pair, 0x4DB7…9d46, is unusable: its "USDT" carries 18 decimals and the pair holds 1.79 WETH against 115 trillion of it, implying a price near 6×1013. A Uniswap V4 PoolManager also exists at 0x6f97…a8b7 and has emitted a V4 Swap event, but only one in the last ~60,000 blocks, so there is nothing there to quote against yet.
